# 📊 Wavecon Telecom — 5G Impact Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the impact of **5G launch on Wavecon Telecom's business performance** using Power BI.

The objective is to compare key business metrics before and after the 5G launch, identify underperforming KPIs and plans, understand customer behavior, and provide data-driven recommendations to telecom stakeholders.

**Role:** Data Analyst
**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI
**Domain:** Telecom / Business Analytics
**Analysis Period:** January–April 2022 (Pre-5G) vs June–September 2022 (Post-5G)
**Implementation Month:** May 2022

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis focuses on the following key questions:

1. What was the impact of the 5G launch on overall revenue?
2. Which KPIs underperformed after the 5G launch?
3. Which plans performed well and which plans declined after 5G?
4. Was any particular plan significantly affected by the 5G launch?
5. Which plans were discontinued after 5G, and what business pattern can be observed?

## 📈 Key KPIs Analyzed

| KPI                               | Pre-5G | Post-5G |  Change |
| --------------------------------- | -----: | ------: | ------: |
| Revenue                           | ₹16.0B |  ₹15.9B |  -0.50% |
| ARPU                              |  190.2 |   211.3 | +11.05% |
| Total Active Users (TAU)          |  84.4M |   77.4M |  -8.28% |
| Total Users using Services (TUsU) |   5.6M |    7.0M | +23.50% |

### 📌 KPI Observation

Although overall revenue remained relatively stable, the post-5G period showed a significant increase in **ARPU** and **TUsU**, while **TAU declined**.

This indicates that revenue stability was accompanied by changes in the customer base and usage behavior.

## 💰 Revenue Analysis

Overall revenue changed from approximately:

**₹16.0B → ₹15.9B**

This represents a decline of approximately **0.50%** in the post-5G period.

However, the relatively small change in total revenue masks differences across individual cities and plans.

### City-level observations

Some cities experienced revenue declines after 5G, while others showed positive movement.

Examples:

* Delhi: **-2.83%**
* Chennai: **-2.59%**
* Ahmedabad: **-2.02%**
* Hyderabad: **-1.29%**
* Mumbai: **+0.31%**
* Bangalore: **+0.75%**
* Jaipur: **+0.98%**
* Raipur: **+1.15%**
* Patna: **+1.48%**
* Gurgaon: **+1.51%**
* Lucknow: **+1.82%**

This suggests that the impact of 5G was not uniform across markets.

## 📦 Plan Performance Analysis

The analysis compared revenue generated by individual plans before and after the 5G launch.

### Strong / Stable Plans

**P1**

* Pre-5G: ₹1.803B
* Post-5G: ₹2.373B

P1 showed substantial growth after the 5G launch.

**P2 and P3**

* Both remained approximately stable across the two periods.

### Plans Showing Decline

Several legacy plans experienced revenue declines.

**P7**

* Pre-5G: ₹582.4M
* Post-5G: ₹155.6M
* Decline: approximately **73.28%**

P7 was one of the most significantly affected plans.

### Discontinued Plans

P8, P9 and P10 showed revenue in the pre-5G period but no revenue in the post-5G period.

At the same time, newer plans such as **P11, P12 and P13** generated significant post-5G revenue.

This pattern is consistent with a portfolio transition toward newer plans, although the available data alone does not establish the exact reason for discontinuation.

---

## 🔎 Key Business Insights

### 1. Revenue remained relatively stable

The 5G launch did not result in a major change in overall revenue during the analyzed periods.

Revenue changed by approximately **-0.50%**.

### 2. ARPU increased

ARPU increased by approximately **11.05%**, indicating higher average revenue per user despite the decline in total active users.

### 3. Active users declined

TAU decreased by approximately **8.28%** after the 5G launch.

This metric should be monitored because customer-base contraction can have longer-term implications.

### 4. Service usage increased

TUsU increased by approximately **23.50%**, suggesting increased engagement among users who continued using services.

### 5. P7 requires further investigation

P7 experienced an approximately **73.28% revenue decline**.

Before discontinuing or completely phasing out the plan, customer migration should be analyzed to determine whether users moved to newer plans or left the network.

### 6. Portfolio transition is visible

The disappearance of revenue from P8, P9 and P10 alongside the introduction of P11, P12 and P13 indicates a significant change in the plan portfolio after 5G.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

### Customer Retention

Monitor the decline in TAU and identify customers at risk of churn.

### Plan Migration Analysis

Investigate whether customers from declining plans such as P7 migrated to newer plans.

### Scale Successful Plans

Evaluate the growth of P1 and newly introduced plans such as P11, P12 and P13.

### City-Level Strategy

Develop targeted strategies for cities showing negative revenue movement rather than applying a single nationwide strategy.

### Monitor 5G Adoption

Track ARPU, active users, service usage and plan migration together to understand the long-term effect of 5G.

### P7 Investigation

Before discontinuation, analyze customer migration, churn and revenue contribution to determine whether the decline represents migration or customer loss.
